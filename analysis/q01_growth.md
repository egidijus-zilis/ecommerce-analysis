# Q1. Is the business growing profitably?

**Question:** Is the business growing profitably? How do revenue, gross profit and order volume change month over month and year over year, and how much of the change comes from new customers, repeat customers and order value?

**Expected:**
- Revenue is about 150–200k € per year, and it is higher every year.
- November and December have the most sales (Black Friday, Christmas). Summer has less.
- The profit margin (profit / revenue) stays about the same every year.
- Most of the growth comes from new customers, not from customers who buy again.

**Result:**

Same period every year (January–September):

| Year | Net revenue | Growth | Orders | Avg order value | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 77,339 € | – | 1,441 | 53.67 € | 39.3 % |
| 2025 | 180,534 € | +133 % | 2,826 | 63.88 € | 38.5 % |
| 2026 | 215,895 € | +20 % | 3,372 | 64.03 € | 39.1 % |

Full years: 2024 – 141,656 €, 2025 – 267,533 €.

Where the growth comes from (January–September, net revenue):

| Year | New customers | Returning customers |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 77,339 € | – |
| 2025 | 145,295 € | 35,240 € |
| 2026 | 151,366 € (+4 %) | 64,529 € (+83 %) |

|---|---|---|---|

- November is the strongest month every year (Black Friday). December is second.
- November has the lowest margin (34 %, other months 37–44 %).

Check against what I expected:
- Revenue about 150–200k € per year: **partly.** 2024 was 142k €, 2025 was 268k € (more than I expected).
- Revenue goes up every year: **yes**, but growth slowed from +133 % to +20 %.
- November and December are the best months: **yes** for November, December is second. Summer is lower in 2025, but not in 2026.
- Margin stays the same: **yes**, about 38–39 % every year. Only November is lower.
- Most growth comes from new customers: **yes in 2025, no in 2026.** In 2026 new customers added only +4 %, and about 83 % of the growth came from returning customers.

**So what:**
- The shop is still growing, but slower: +133 % in 2025, +20 % in 2026. This is normal after a strong start from a small base.
- In 2026 almost all growth came from returning customers. Revenue from new customers grew only 4 %, so the shop is not getting many more new customers than a year ago. If this does not change, growth will slow down more. Next step: check which marketing channels bring new customers and what they cost (Q9, Q10).
- The margin is stable at about 38–39 %, so the shop grows without losing profit per sale. In November the margin is lower because of discounts, but November still brings the most profit in euros. Whether these discounts pay off is checked in Q4.

**Limitations:**
- 2026 has data only until September, so I compared January–September of every year.
- Profit uses today's product cost. Older costs are not known, so profit in 2024 may be a bit off.
- A customer counts as "new" for the whole year of their first order. So their second and third orders that year are also counted as "new".
